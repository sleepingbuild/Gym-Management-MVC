## 1. Introduction

Gym Management System is a web-based application designed to help gym owners and members manage gym operations efficiently.

### System Roles
- **Member**: Regular gym member
- **Trainer**: Gym trainer who coaches members
- **Admin**: System administrator

## 2. Getting Started

### 2.1 System Requirements
- Web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge)
- Internet connection
- Valid email address

### 2.2 Access the System
1. Open browser and navigate to: `http://localhost:5225`
2. Click **Đăng nhập** to login or **Đăng ký** to register

### 2.3 Registration
1. Click **Đăng ký**
2. Fill in all required fields:
   - Full Name
   - Email
   - Phone Number
   - Password
   - Confirm Password
3. Click **Tạo tài khoản**
4. You will be automatically logged in

## 3. User Guide

### 3.1 Member Functions

#### 3.1.1 View Membership Packages
1. Navigate to **Gói tập** page
2. View available packages with prices and descriptions
3. Click **Đăng ký** to purchase

#### 3.1.2 Register for Membership
1. Navigate to **Gói tập của tôi**
2. Click **Đăng ký gói tập**
3. Select a package
4. Click **Đăng ký ngay**
5. Complete payment via VNPay

#### 3.1.3 Book a Training Session
1. Navigate to **Đặt lịch**
2. Click **Đặt lịch mới**
3. Select:
   - Trainer
   - Date
   - Time slot
4. Add notes (optional)
5. Click **Xác nhận đặt lịch**

#### 3.1.4 View Booking History
1. Navigate to **Lịch sử đặt lịch**
2. Filter by date or status
3. View all bookings

#### 3.1.5 Track Workout Progress
1. Navigate to **Tiến trình tập luyện**
2. Click **Ghi nhận mới**
3. Enter:
   - Weight
   - Height
   - Body Fat %
   - Muscle Mass
   - Waist Circumference
4. Add notes (optional)
5. Click **Lưu tiến trình**

#### 3.1.6 Use AI Assistant
1. Navigate to **Trợ lý AI**
2. Type your question in the chat box
3. Click **Send**
4. AI will respond with helpful information

#### 3.1.7 View Payment History
1. Navigate to **Thanh toán**
2. Click **Lịch sử**
3. View all transactions

---

### 3.2 Trainer Functions

#### 3.2.1 View Schedule
1. Login as Trainer
2. Navigate to **Lịch làm việc**
3. View assigned bookings

#### 3.2.2 Confirm Bookings
1. Login as Trainer
2. Navigate to **Đặt lịch**
3. View pending bookings
4. Click **Xác nhận** to confirm

## 4. Admin Guide

### 4.1 Manage Membership Packages
1. Login as Admin
2. Navigate to **Quản lý gói tập**
3. Actions:
   - **Add**: Fill in form and click **Lưu**
   - **Edit**: Click **Sửa** and update
   - **Delete**: Click **Xóa** and confirm
   - **Toggle Status**: Click **Khóa/Mở khóa**

### 4.2 Manage Trainers
1. Login as Admin
2. Navigate to **Quản lý huấn luyện viên**
3. Actions:
   - **Add**: Fill in form and click **Lưu**
   - **Edit**: Click **Sửa** and update
   - **Delete**: Click **Xóa** and confirm
   - **Toggle Availability**: Click **Tạm nghỉ/Hoạt động**

### 4.3 Manage Trainer Schedules
1. Login as Admin
2. Navigate to **Lịch làm việc**
3. Actions:
   - **Add**: Select trainer, day, time
   - **Edit**: Update schedule
   - **Delete**: Remove schedule

### 4.4 View Dashboard
1. Login as Admin
2. Navigate to **Dashboard**
3. View:
   - Revenue statistics
   - Membership analytics
   - Booking analytics
   - Trainer performance
   - Payment analytics

### 4.5 View All Bookings
1. Login as Admin
2. Navigate to **Quản lý đặt lịch**
3. View all bookings
4. Cancel bookings if needed

### 4.6 View Payment History
1. Login as Admin
2. Navigate to **Lịch sử thanh toán**
3. View all transactions
4. Export to CSV if needed

## 5. FAQs

### Q: How do I reset my password?
A: Contact the admin to reset your password.

### Q: How do I renew my membership?
A: Go to **Gói tập của tôi**, find your active membership, and click **Gia hạn**.

### Q: What payment methods are supported?
A: VNPay (credit/debit cards, banking apps).

### Q: Can I cancel a booking?
A: Yes, go to your booking and click **Hủy**.

### Q: How do I contact my trainer?
A: Use the AI Assistant or add notes when booking.

### Q: What if I miss a session?
A: You can cancel up to 24 hours before the session. Contact your trainer for rescheduling.

### Q: How do I export payment history?
A: Go to **Lịch sử thanh toán** and click **Xuất CSV**.

### Q: What does the AI Assistant do?
A: Provides fitness advice, workout recommendations, nutrition tips, and answers gym-related questions.
